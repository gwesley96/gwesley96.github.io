About
======
I am a second-year mathematics PhD student at [The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu/), advised by [David Penneys](https://people.math.osu.edu/penneys.2/). I completed my undergrarduate studies at the [University of Notre Dame](https://www.nd.edu/) in 2023, where I majored in Honors Mathematics, Physics, and Philosophy. I wrote my undergraduate senior thesis under the guidance of [Brian C. Hall](https://math.nd.edu/people/faculty/brian-c-hall/).

Research
======
My interests lie in the intersection of mathematical physics, (higher) category theory, and algebraic topology.
